Step 01

Who's the opponent drafted?

Add every mon on the enemy team. This is the full gauntlet your candidate set gets checked against.

One per line. "(T)" for Tera is ignored automatically.

Step 02

Which of your mons are we building?

Pick one Pokémon from your draft. Everything downstream — moves, EVs, items — is scoped to this mon.

Step 03

Which moves are in play?

Nothing's checked by default — turn on only what you actually want tested. Star a move to lock it into every generated set.

Step 04

EV assumptions

Lock any stat you already know (like a speed-creep number). Everything else gets explored at 0 or 252 — this tool finds the best moves for a role, not a fully-optimized spread.

Step 05

Items to test

Search and pick 3-4 — every item multiplies the number of sets generated.

Step 06

Assumed opponent threats

The evaluator assumes worst-case counterplay: every damaging move any known set runs, paired with whichever of that mon's real set spreads is worst for you. Scan for a mon whose pool is missing a move you'd want covered — add it to gen9_filtered.js and re-run.

Report

Win-con breakdown

Shaded by KO quality — a sweep can't trade damage every turn. The +N badge is how many boosts that KO needs; the pt badge is the mon's draft cost, and the set score weights pricier mons and taxes every extra boost. Ordered heaviest-first. Every calc uses the max damage roll; chip and hazards aren't modelled.
